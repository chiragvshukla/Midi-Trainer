microGM SoundFont — microGM.sf2
===============================

This file ships inside MidiTrainer and is loaded by default so the app plays
without any network access. It is a complete General MIDI bank (128 melodic
presets plus drum kits).

Author: "michael" (musical-artifacts.com/artifacts/2529)
Source: the author's Google Drive mirror of microgm-opt.sf2.
